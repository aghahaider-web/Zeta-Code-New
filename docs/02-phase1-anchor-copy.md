# Phase 1 — Anchor Copy Draft
## Primary Brand Message

**Headline:** Stop settling for brochure websites.

**Supporting proposition:**
ZetaCode builds strategy-led websites for service businesses that need to earn trust, clarify value, and generate better enquiries.

## Positioning Statement (internal reference, not public copy)
ZetaCode is an agency-led conversion website studio for owners and founders of ambitious service businesses — construction and trades, property and interiors, health and clinics, with adjacent fit for gyms, law firms, and select professional services. The website is treated as a sales asset, not a portfolio piece. Custom website projects start from $1,500, with focused delivery typically in 2–3 weeks depending on scope, content, and approval speed.

## Hero Eyebrow Options (for later design-system selection)
1. For service businesses that are done blending in.
2. Websites built to convert, not just exist.
3. A sales asset, not a brochure.

## Voice Check — Approved vs Forbidden
Approved tone: direct, commercially reasoned, respectful challenge, short sentences, International English, outcome-led.

Forbidden phrases (never use): "We are passionate about…", "One-stop solution", "Best agency", "Revolutionary", "Guaranteed first-page rankings", "We transform businesses" (without evidence).

## Notes
This is anchor copy only — full homepage, service, and industry copy is Phase 2 work per the delivery sequence. No technical/architecture decisions are made in this document.
