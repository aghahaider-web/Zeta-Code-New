# Phase 2 — Service Page Copy: SEO
Route: /services/seo

## What it is
Search engine optimisation for service businesses, focused on qualified
organic visibility over 6–12 months — not a guaranteed "first page" promise.

## Who it's for
Businesses that already have (or are building) a conversion-ready website
and want to grow qualified organic traffic over time.

## The business problem it solves
Traffic without qualification wastes everyone's time. Generic SEO chases
volume; this focuses on the searches your actual buyers use.

## What's included
- Technical SEO foundations (ZetaCode, in-house)
- Keyword and intent strategy
- On-page optimisation guidance
- Ongoing content and authority-building delivery
- Monthly reporting and review

## What's not included
- Guaranteed rankings, traffic, or lead volume — no legitimate provider can promise this
- Paid acquisition (see /services/paid-acquisition)

## Delivery model — Partner Disclosure
ZetaCode owns strategy, quality control, reporting, and client
communication for this service. Ongoing SEO execution is delivered by
vetted specialist partners working under ZetaCode's direction. You have one
point of contact throughout — ZetaCode — regardless of who executes the
work behind the scenes. This is not performed in-house, and we don't
represent it as such.

## Typical timeline
Meaningful organic results typically build over 6–12 months. Reporting
begins from month one.

## FAQ
**Why isn't SEO done fully in-house?**
Because specialist SEO execution is a distinct discipline from website
strategy and build. Rather than doing it adequately ourselves, we manage
vetted specialists so you get focused expertise — with ZetaCode still
owning quality, reporting, and communication.

**Can you guarantee first-page rankings?**
No. Anyone guaranteeing that is either wrong or not being straight with
you. We can commit to strategy, transparency, and consistent measurement.
