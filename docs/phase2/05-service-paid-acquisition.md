# Phase 2 — Service Page Copy: Paid Acquisition
Route: /services/paid-acquisition

## What it is
Paid search and social acquisition campaigns for service businesses,
managed end-to-end by ZetaCode.

## Who it's for
Businesses with a conversion-ready website that want faster, budget-driven
lead volume alongside organic growth.

## The business problem it solves
Paid traffic sent to a weak page wastes budget. This service pairs paid
acquisition with a site already built to convert.

## What's included
- Campaign strategy and audience targeting
- Ad creative direction and copy
- Budget management and optimisation
- Monthly performance reporting

## What's not included
- Website build (see /services/conversion-websites)
- Guaranteed cost-per-lead or return figures

## Delivery model — Partner Disclosure
ZetaCode owns strategy, quality control, reporting, and client
communication. Campaigns are executed by vetted specialist partners under
ZetaCode's direction. One point of contact throughout. This is not
performed in-house, and we don't represent it as such.

## Typical timeline
Campaigns typically launch within 1–2 weeks of approved strategy and
budget. Performance data becomes meaningful after the first few weeks.

## FAQ
**Can you guarantee a specific cost-per-lead?**
No. Costs vary by industry, market, and competition. We report actual
numbers transparently rather than promising a figure upfront.

**Do you require a minimum ad budget?**
Confirmed during discovery based on market and goals.
