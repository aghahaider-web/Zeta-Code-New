# Phase 2 — Industry Page: Construction & Trades
Route: /industries/construction-trades

## Buyer concerns
Homeowners and commercial clients want proof of licensing, past work
quality, and reliability before they'll trust a contractor with a large
job. Price shopping is common; trust shortens the list fast.

## Common website/conversion failures
- Photo galleries with no context on scope or challenge
- No clear service-area or project-type filtering
- Quote requests buried behind generic contact forms
- No trust signals visible before the fold (licensing, insurance, experience)

## Decision journey
Research online → compare 3–5 contractors by portfolio and reviews →
request quotes from a shortlist → decide on trust + price + availability.

## Trust requirements
Visible licensing/insurance info, real project photos with context,
clear service areas, responsive quote process.

## Quote/appointment flow recommendation
Structured quote-request form (project type, scope, timeline, budget band)
rather than a generic "contact us" field — mirrors the proposal system in
Section 6.3.

## Relevant services
Conversion websites, SEO (local trade search intent), Care plan

Book a discovery call to scope a site built around how homeowners actually
choose a contractor.
