# Phase 2 — Service Page Copy: Conversion Websites (Flagship)
Route: /services/conversion-websites

## What it is
A custom website built around one goal: turning the right visitors into
qualified conversations. Strategy, structure, and message come before
visual design.

## Who it's for
Owners and founders of service businesses — construction and trades,
property and interiors, health and clinics, and adjacent-fit sectors like
gyms, law firms, and select professional services — who are ready to stop
relying on a website that just exists.

## The problem it solves
A generic website doesn't lose you obvious prospects — it loses you the
ones who almost chose you. It fails to clarify value fast enough, fails to
build trust before attention runs out, and gives visitors no clear next
step.

## What's included
- Discovery and messaging strategy
- Information architecture and user journey mapping
- Custom visual design (editorial, premium, credible)
- Full development build (Next.js, fast and accessible)
- Technical and on-page SEO foundations
- Booking and/or proposal conversion flow
- Launch QA: accessibility, performance, SEO, security

## What's not included

## FAQ
**How is this different from a template website?**
Templates start with a layout and fit your content into it. We start with
your buyer's decision journey and build the structure around that — so the
site is arguing your case, not just displaying it.

**What if I don't have final content ready?**
We can work with a content-readiness plan during discovery, but delivery
timelines are directly tied to how quickly content and approvals come back.

**Do you guarantee results?**
No. We don't promise guaranteed rankings, leads, or revenue — anyone who
does is selling you a claim they can't back up. We build the site to give
you the best realistic chance at better enquiries, and we're transparent
about what's proven versus projected.

**Is $1,500 the final price?**
No — it's the starting point for the simplest scope. Final investment
depends on your specific requirements, confirmed after discovery.
