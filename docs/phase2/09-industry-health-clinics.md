# Phase 2 — Industry Page: Health & Clinics
Route: /industries/health-clinics

## Buyer concerns
Patients need reassurance on legitimacy, practitioner credentials, and
ease of booking. Health decisions carry higher trust sensitivity than
most service categories.

## Common website/conversion failures
- Booking buried behind phone-only contact
- No visible practitioner credentials or clinic information
- Generic medical stock imagery that reduces perceived authenticity
- Unclear service/treatment descriptions

## Decision journey
Search for symptom/treatment or clinic name → check credibility and
reviews → check appointment availability → book.

## Trust requirements
Visible practitioner credentials, clear treatment descriptions, transparent
appointment process. No health/patient data collected via public forms
(Section 6.5 constraint — booking system stays non-clinical, admin-managed
slots only).

## Quote/appointment flow recommendation
Discovery-call or appointment-request booking flow — no patient health
data captured through public forms.

## Relevant services
Conversion websites, SEO (treatment/local search intent), Care plan

## CTA
Book a discovery call to scope a site that makes booking simple and
credibility immediate.
