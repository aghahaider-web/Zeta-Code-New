# Phase 2 — Service Page Copy: Care Plan
Route: /care-plan

## What it is
Optional ongoing support that keeps a launched website technically
reliable and commercially effective.

## Who it's for
Clients who've launched with ZetaCode and want the site to stay fast,
secure, and conversion-focused without managing it themselves.

## The business problem it solves
Websites decay — content goes stale, dependencies age, conversion paths
get overlooked as the business changes. The care plan catches this before
it costs enquiries.

## What's included
- Technical monitoring and updates
- Performance and security checks
- Conversion review and improvement recommendations
- Priority response for fixes

## What's not included
- New page/section builds beyond agreed scope (quoted separately)
- SEO/paid acquisition management (see respective service pages)

## Delivery model
Ongoing monthly engagement, managed directly by ZetaCode.

## Typical timeline
Starts immediately after launch; ongoing for as long as active.

## Starting investment
Confirmed at proposal stage based on site scope and care level required.

## FAQ
**Is the care plan required?**
No — it's optional. Without it, the site remains functional but won't
receive proactive monitoring or improvement.

**Can I cancel?**
Terms confirmed at signup; contact the team through your lead record.
